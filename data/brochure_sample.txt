About the Project
Meridian Heights is a premium high-rise residential development by Skyline Developers, spread across 6.2
acres in Neopolis, Kokapet, at the heart of Hyderabad's western growth corridor. The project comprises two
residential towers, Tower A and Tower B, rising to 24 floors each, together offering 6 available residences
across 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations at the time of this brochure's printing, ranging from 1,150 to 2,500 sq. ft.
Meridian Heights is designed for professionals and families seeking proximity to Hyderabad's Financial District
without compromising on open space, amenities, or long-term appreciation potential in one of the city's
fastest-developing micro-markets.
